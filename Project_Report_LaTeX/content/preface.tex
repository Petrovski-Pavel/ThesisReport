\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This report presents the work carried out as part of the Master thesis project in
Cyber-Physical Systems at HAN University of Applied Sciences. The project was conducted
in collaboration with Ambyon and HAN Automotive Research.

The objective of the thesis is to design and evaluate a scalable computer-vision
pipeline for interaction-point perception in hospital environments. All work
described in this report was performed by the author.
