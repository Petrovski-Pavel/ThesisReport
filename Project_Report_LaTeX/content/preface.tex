\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

This report presents the work carried out for the Master Engineering Systems
programme at HAN University of Applied Sciences, within the Cyber-Physical
Systems track and its embedded control and big-data/small-data modules. The
thesis was conducted in collaboration with Ambyon and HAN Automotive Research
and focused on the development of a computer-vision pipeline for autonomous
robot interaction with elevator panels.

Working on this project was both a technical and personal challenge. It required
combining computer vision, embedded deployment and
structured evaluation into one coherent system. At the same time, it gave me
the opportunity to work on a practical problem with clear real-world relevance,
which made the project especially rewarding.

I would like to thank my supervisors for their guidance, critical feedback, and
continued support throughout the project. I also thank the people at Ambyon and
HAN Automotive Research for their collaboration and for providing the context in
which this work could be carried out. I am equally grateful to my colleagues and
management at ICT Strypes for their support and understanding while this thesis
was running alongside my professional responsibilities. In addition, I thank my
friends and my parents for their support during the thesis process, and
especially my girlfriend for her patience, encouragement, and belief in me
throughout the more demanding periods of this work. Finally, I would like to
thank myself for staying committed to the project and carrying it through to
completion.
